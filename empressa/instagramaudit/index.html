08 — Before we scale anything

Clicks aren't customers. Three checks first.

₹1–2 per visit is unusually cheap for fashion. That can mean good ads, or it can mean accidental taps and low-quality placements. The ad tools can't tell us which. The store can.

CHECK 1

Do the visits exist?

Compare Shopify sessions from Instagram with the 7,361 website visits Meta reported for the same dates.

If sessions are far lower, clicks aren't landing.
CHECK 2

Did anyone buy?

Pull add-to-carts and orders by UTM or campaign to get cost per purchase. That's the number the budget should follow.

No UTMs? Add them before relaunch.
CHECK 3

Did they stay?

Check bounce rate and time on site for Instagram traffic, and whether any spend ran on Audience Network placements.

Under 10 seconds means low-intent traffic.

The results also span different periods (ad 12 ran in August 2024), so seasonality and product launches may skew direct comparisons.

Four moves for the next ₹50k.

Set up purchase tracking

Meta pixel and Conversions API on Shopify, plus UTMs on every ad. Without this, no campaign can optimise for sales.
